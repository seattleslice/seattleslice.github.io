Seattle Loves Indie Creators Expo

A one-day B2B conference built to empower indie and AA game developers.

September 2, 2026 • McCaw Hall, Seattle

Created For Indie Devs, By Indie Devs

SLICE is designed to provide practical tools, strategic insights, and essential connections for game developers - whether you're just getting started or leading a growing studio.

Expect a full day featuring panels, microtalks, roundtables, and networking with hundreds of local and international industry professionals. Topics span funding, marketing, team-building, and more.

Venue

Marion Oliver McCaw Hall - 321 Mercer St, Seattle, WA 98109

Stairs Elevator Balcony

Convenient parking is $20 at the skybridge-attached garage on the opposite side of Mercer Street.
Free public street parking is available within a 10 minute walk to the north.

Free street parking, no time limit Free, but two hours only Other paid lots

The unrestricted blocks start north of Ward St, and it is uphill the whole way. The Mercer Street Garage entrance is on 3rd Ave N between Mercer St and Roy St. Street parking rules change block to block, so always read the posted signs.

McCaw Hall sits on the north edge of the Seattle Center campus, about two miles north of downtown. The Monorail is the quickest way in from downtown, and several bus routes stop within a few blocks.

Monorail - easiest from downtown

The Seattle Center Monorail runs between Westlake Center (5th & Pine, 3rd floor) and Seattle Center Station. The ride is about 3 minutes and trains leave every 8 to 10 minutes. From the station it is roughly a 7 minute walk north across the campus to the hall. One way is $4. Weekday service runs 7:30am to 11:00pm.

Link light rail

Westlake is the closest Link station to Seattle Center. There is elevator access from the Link platform straight up to the Monorail, so the transfer is short. Walking the last stretch instead takes about 25 minutes.

Bus

Queen Anne Ave N & Mercer St is a 5 minute walk west of the hall and is served by routes 1, 2, 8, 13, 32 and RapidRide D.

Routes 3 and 4 stop two blocks east, at 5th Ave N & Mercer St southbound and 5th Ave N & Republican St northbound.

Biking and rideshare

Bike racks are on Mercer St by the Stage Door entrance, under the skybridge, and across from the main north entrance. Rideshare pick-up and drop-off zones are marked on Mercer, Republican, Harrison and John.
